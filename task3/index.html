About LeBron James

LeBron Raymone James Sr. was born on December 30, 1984, in Akron, Ohio. Widely regarded as one of the greatest basketball players in history, he entered the NBA directly out of high school as the first overall pick in the 2003 draft. Over his legendary career, he revolutionized the position-less era of basketball, combining elite playmaking, athleticism, and scoring dominance. In 2023, he broke Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's record to become the NBA's all-time leading scorer.

Important Career Milestones

  1. 2003:Drafted 1st overall by the Cleveland Cavaliers and wins NBA Rookie of the Year.
  2. 2012:Wins his first NBA Championship and Finals MVP with the Miami Heat.
  3. 2016:Leads the Cleveland Cavaliers to an iconic 3-1 comeback victory in the NBA Finals.
  4. 2020:Captures his fourth NBA Championship with the Los Angeles Lakers.
  5. 2023: Surpasses Kareem Abdul-Jabbar as the NBA's all-time leading scorer.

Key Achievements & Contributions

  • 4× NBA Champion and 4× NBA Finals MVP
  • 4× NBA Most Valuable Player (MVP)
  • Founded the I PROMISE School in Akron, Ohio for under-served children
  • First active player in NBA history to reach $1 billion in net worth
